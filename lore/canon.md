# YTIR: Echoes Eternal — Canon

Ruled by Adam Keller. Everything below is decided; contradictions in older
material (repos, prototypes, the v2.0 site rewrite) are overridden by this file.

## Identity

- **YTIR** (all caps) is simultaneously the game's name, an in-world entity,
  and the rules system itself. Pronounced "Yatir".
- Name origin: "singularity" reversed → *ytiralugnis*, shortened to its first
  four letters.
- YTIR is triune: a being, a place, and life itself.
- Spelling, naming, and pronunciation of words are load-bearing design
  elements, not flavor. Entity names are built from sound + deconstruction
  (reversals/anagrams) carrying lore meaning.

## Cosmology

- YTIR is an **ouroboric entity** — eternally living, dying, and being reborn
  across iterations of space and time.
- Outer forces from the outer worlds exploited weaknesses in its iterations
  (**the hijacking**). In defense, Ytir split into **primes** (expansion of
  itself) and **paradoxes** (collapse of itself).
- Paradoxes are not evil or corrupt — they are the necessary opposing force,
  like a black hole's event horizon balancing its singularity.
- **Ytir is the balance** of Lumvaren and Neravmul together — not a third
  being above them. Both incarnates hold significant power.

## The incarnates

- **Lumvaren** — the Prime Incarnate. Named itself **Luminara** — the heart
  prime. Sings the **song of emotion**; creation as music, chords of life.
  Created therein: the heart constellation Luminara, Vara Shryn (the
  law-bearer), Revyen, the Fractal Feather. Thesis line: *"What was sung
  shall be sung again."*
- **Neravmul** — the Paradox Incarnate. Exact letter reversal of Lumvaren.
- Luminara's pronouns are **lur/lurs** (neopronoun), not she/her. Whether the
  other primes/paradoxes have their own pronouns is unresolved.

## The three prime emotions

- **Heart** = the relationships/connections within the moment.
- **Dread** = the weight/gravity — the moment is fleeting and may end; fear
  that protects you or freezes you in place.
- **Chaos** = expansion/oscillation — the quantum nature of the moment
  rippling outward in time.

Each prime emotion has an **antithesis**:
- heart ↔ despair/loneliness (absence of connection)
- dread ↔ death/destruction (absence of protective fear)
- chaos ↔ order (order = true unmaking, the void — opposite of pure creation)

Neravmul hid in shadow holding these antitheses, waiting for the perfect
moment to strike in the next song of creation (era).

  Soul Shiver Wastes, Veiled Eventide — tentative.

## The falls

- Eras pass as primes tend their dominions; some begin to fall — a corruption
  around "being their self". Falls come from an emotion in its purest form
  without the checks and balances of the other two.
- The **first fall** is a blink, not a fall: the emergence of **Neyver**
  (N-E-Y-V-E-R, exact reversal of Revyen; evokes "never") — retroactive
  non-existence (never born, not merely dead), herald of the end of the
  singularity. Neyver is part of Neravmul's identity, yet Neravmul fears it —
  kryptonite to intentionality itself (Revyen is Lumvaren's kryptonite in
  parallel).

## The fracturing and the players

- In the final eras, primes/paradoxes split and multiply, each holding
  regions of space/power/pressure; their gifts and imbalances begin the
  collapse. Each prime/paradoxum fractures off parts of its true emotion as
  **Aspects** — lesser and more mortal than the true echoes, with iteration
  amnesia (parts linger, parts forget), more corruptible.
- End state: all primes fallen, paradoxes whole again, power imbalanced; a
  corrupted one rises — originally an "antibody" against something much worse
  (the outer forces), now power-hungry, having seen visions of its own demise
  and decided to become immortal.
- The players are the **Echo Touched** — born with powers reaching beyond the
  stars and beyond iterations, tasked with regaining balance and restoring
  YTIR to its proper glory.

## Echo (the home plane)

- The human world / home plane is called **Echo** — the common name. Nohpitna
  remains the forgotten true name (deep lore); Echo is what people call it.
- The world's name was hiding inside "Echo Touched" all along — the people
  were named for the world before the world was named.
- "ohce" (Echo reversed) deliberately doesn't work — Echo resists reversal
  (a reflection of a reflection is only static). That is why it's the human
  world: where perfect symmetries break down and the imperfect Echo Touched
  defy prediction by the perfect beings.

## "Echoes Eternal" (design ethos)

- Everything is an echo — each echo holds the original singularity plus
  distorted reverberations, and each echo is itself a singularity (recursive).
- Blends real physics/metaphysics (sound, light, gravity) into an
  emotion-driven TTRPG.
- **Fail-forward**: a failed roll is a complication that moves the narrative,
  never a dead end. Built to make players take risks and tell stories.
- **Echo** (the design concept) is an emotional mirror — the environment
  teaches the endgame (hold heart, dread, chaos in balance) without tutorial
  lines, nudging players toward equilibrium.

## The six classes (preliminary — structure not final)

2 per emotion:
- **Heart** — Warden, Luminary
- **Dread** — Carrion Knight, Adjudicator
- **Chaos** — Dreamstitcher, Rift Blade

- 6 MORE classes embodying the Paradoxum are in the works, but structure is
  NOT settled (may be 2-per-emotion, possibly 1; needs playtesting). Do not
  treat a 12-class total as final.
- **Class lattice** (ruled Sep 22, 2026): each class has a pair (same-emotion
  partner) and a twin (cross-emotion neighbor), forming a ring:
  Warden — Carrion Knight — Adjudicator — Riftblade — Dreamstitcher —
  Luminary — back to Warden. Twin links: Warden↔Carrion Knight (Heart-Dread),
  Luminary↔Dreamstitcher (Heart-Chaos), Adjudicator↔Riftblade (Dread-Chaos);
  each emotion touches both others exactly once.
- **Luminary** (heart): phoenix battle-healer — healer and damage dealer,
  star-themed (burns like a star, heals like Luminara). True purpose:
  extinguish the corruption in Aranimul the Paradoxum, bind the heart
  resonance of iterations through the reverb (echo), and guide the party
  toward balance of heart.
- **Adjudicator** (dread): like a Quincy, a ranger, or a lawyer — the
  inquisitor who gets behind enemy lines or the judge-jury-executioner; they
  'mark' targets, reducing GM pressure; ranged or very personal playstyle.

## The hero's journey and endgame (canon Sep 20)

- Opens on an island village on Echo; the Cult of the Blighted Heart awakens
  Aranimul, who lays waste to the village; the hero flees by boat with a
  target on his back.
- Hero: **Kalos Ignus Hearthgaurd** (Warden). Friends: **Gwendolyn Zinnie
  Foster** (Adjudicator, nature ranger) and **Atlas 'Ace' Coswell Emberstien**
  (Dreamstitcher).
- The end (collapse) MUST happen — the choice is HOW: a purification race to
  redeem some of the Paradoxum before the primes all fall (the Great
  Inversion prophecy: *"the Paradoxum Redeemed… purified by the Light of
  Understanding"*).
- The hero ends holding all three emotions in balance — a little Ytir — and
  meets Ytir in a dream/trance, realizing the Lumvaren/Neravmul balance was
  inside them all along.

## Other ruled items

- **Save-point design** (from the video-game concept): the cracked glowing
  stone IS Ytir / the Echo Touched; the save screen is a pool of water —
  saving = dropping the stone into the pool (the pool shifts with how the
  game is going), resuming = picking the stone back up with the scene changed.
- **Book series**: the lore is being written as both a Hobbit-style hero
  entry AND a Silmarillion-style mythic history (*"Every story needs a hero.
  Every hero has a history."*). The Echo Touched bloodline + iteration
  amnesia let the hero discover the myth alongside the reader.
- **Homepage hero**: the v1 Ytir artwork is "the unified face" of the brand.
